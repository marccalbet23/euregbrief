Why now

DIWASS is already changing cross-border waste operations.

The digital transition is underway, but the practical impact differs by procedure, operator role and Member State. EU Reg Brief turns regulatory changes into operational actions.

21 May 2026

DIWASS became mandatory for notification and movement documents involving an EU Member State. Prior informed consent procedures are now handled digitally.

1 Jan 2027

The transitional approach for Annex VII ends after 31 December 2026, making readiness for green-listed waste flows a key operational priority.
